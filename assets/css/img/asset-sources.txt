Product photos (assets/img/katalog/):
All 22 bicycle cards and 14 spare-part cards use a different image. The images
were prepared from photographs already present in this project (assets/img/ and
assets/img/catalog-v3/) and from assets/img/produk/ for spare parts. Each file was
trimmed of excess white margin, fitted to a 900 x 675 px (4:3) white canvas, and
saved as WebP quality 90, so every card image has the same dimensions and visual
scale. Original files were kept unchanged.

Prices:
Card prices are market averages collected online in November 2026 from brand
websites (United Bike, Decathlon Indonesia), a Polygon price article, and
marketplace listings. Prices are shown as single values without notes or source
links on the visitor-facing page. Confirm with the shop before publishing.
